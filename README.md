### Hi, I'm Nikita
 
Gameplay programmer working in Unreal Engine 5 — C++ and Blueprints. Computer Science student in Warsaw.
 
I build gameplay systems: the Gameplay Ability System, world subsystems, data-driven content, and the UI that exposes all of it. Right now I am working on **Garden Keeper**, a first-person tower defense where the player never attacks — you plant and rearrange a garden from inside it, and the garden does the fighting.
